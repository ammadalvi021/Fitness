# Exercise Video-Generation Prompts

> **Professional-review gate (read first):** Any footage generated from these
> prompts — or obtained any other way — must be reviewed by a qualified
> fitness professional (certified trainer, physiotherapist, or sports-medicine
> clinician) BEFORE it is published or linked in `EXERCISE_MEDIA`. Wrong demos
> teach unsafe technique. If the reviewer flags a cue or range of motion as
> off, re-generate or re-film; do not "fix it in the edit." Never claim a
> video exists until the entry is actually filled in the registry.

How to use each prompt:

- **Subject:** one adult of average build, athletic wear, neutral gym or home
  backdrop, no brand logos visible.
- **Framing:** full body in frame for standing moves; the working joint(s)
  clearly visible for isolated moves. Well-lit, tripod-steady, no cuts
  mid-rep — each clip should show at least 3 complete, continuous reps.
- **Output:** silent or royalty-free ambient only (no coaching voiceover in
  the clip itself; the app's written instructions carry the coaching).
- Each prompt below is tailored to its exercise — do not substitute a
  generic "person exercising" prompt.

## Chest

### 1. push-up
- **Movement pattern:** horizontal push (prone plank, hands under shoulders).
- **Equipment:** none (mat optional).
- **Camera angle:** side view, slightly elevated, full body in frame.
- **Motion path / tempo:** lower chest toward floor over ~2 s, pause just above floor, press up over ~1 s; 3 full reps continuous.
- **Key form cues:** rigid straight line head→heels, elbows ~45° from torso, chest touches between the hands.
- **Must be visible:** neutral spine throughout, hips never sagging or piking.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** flared 90° elbows, head dropping, partial range.

### 2. incline-push-up
- **Movement pattern:** horizontal push at an incline (hands on a sturdy bench/box, body angled).
- **Equipment:** sturdy bench or box (~knee height).
- **Camera angle:** side view showing the incline angle and full body.
- **Motion path / tempo:** controlled 2 s down to chest near the bench edge, 1 s up; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** straight line head→heels, hands under shoulders on the edge, same push-up mechanics at reduced load.
- **Must be visible:** the bench height and the body's inclined straight line.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** sagging hips, shrugging shoulders toward ears.

### 3. dumbbell-bench-press
- **Movement pattern:** horizontal press with dumbbells, lying on a flat bench.
- **Equipment:** flat bench, pair of dumbbells.
- **Camera angle:** 45° front-side view, upper body and bench in frame.
- **Motion path / tempo:** lower dumbbells to chest sides over 2 s, press up and slightly inward over 1 s without clanging; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** shoulder blades pinned back, slight arch in lower back, feet flat on floor, dumbbells track over the chest (not the face).
- **Must be visible:** independent arm paths, wrists stacked over elbows.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** bouncing dumbbells off the chest, elbows flaring to 90°, feet lifting.

### 4. barbell-bench-press
- **Movement pattern:** horizontal press with a barbell, lying on a flat bench.
- **Equipment:** flat bench, barbell, rack (spotter recommended visually).
- **Camera angle:** side view at torso height plus a second front-angle clip.
- **Motion path / tempo:** unrack, lower bar to mid-chest over 2 s with control, press up over 1 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** five points of contact (head, shoulders, glutes, both feet), bar touches mid-chest, elbows ~45–75°.
- **Must be visible:** the bar path (slight arc back over the shoulders at lockout), full lockout without hyperextending.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** bouncing the bar off the chest, lifting the hips off the bench, uneven bar.

### 5. chest-fly-machine
- **Movement pattern:** seated chest fly (pec-deck), arms in a wide arc.
- **Equipment:** pec-deck / chest-fly machine, seat set so handles align with mid-chest.
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, handles and torso in frame.
- **Motion path / tempo:** open arms wide with a soft elbow bend over 2 s until a chest stretch, squeeze handles together over 1 s without touching; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** slight constant elbow bend, shoulders down and back, chest does the work — not the shoulders.
- **Must be visible:** the wide arc path and the squeeze at the center.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** fully straight locked elbows, shoulders shrugging, slamming the stack.

## Back

### 6. pull-up
- **Movement pattern:** vertical pull, overhand grip, hanging from a bar.
- **Equipment:** pull-up bar (doorway or rack-mounted).
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, full hang to chin-over-bar visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** dead hang, pull chest toward bar over ~1.5 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** initiate by depressing the shoulder blades, chin clears the bar, no kicking or swinging.
- **Must be visible:** full range from dead hang to chin over bar.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** kipping/swinging, half reps, craning the neck over the bar.

### 7. lat-pulldown
- **Movement pattern:** vertical pull on a cable machine, seated.
- **Equipment:** lat-pulldown station, wide bar, thigh pad secured.
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, bar travel and torso visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** pull bar to upper chest over 1.5 s, control up over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** chest tall, slight lean back (~10°), bar to upper chest — never behind the neck.
- **Must be visible:** shoulder blades squeezing down at the bottom.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** behind-the-neck pulldown, excessive swinging lean, shrugging.

### 8. bent-over-barbell-row
- **Movement pattern:** hip-hinged horizontal row with a barbell.
- **Equipment:** barbell.
- **Camera angle:** side view showing the ~45° torso angle and bar path.
- **Motion path / tempo:** row bar to lower ribs over 1.5 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** flat back, torso stable at ~45°, bar touches lower ribcage, squeeze shoulder blades.
- **Must be visible:** the neutral spine and the stable torso angle (no bouncing).
- **Faults to avoid showing:** rounding the lower back, jerking the weight up, standing upright mid-set.

### 9. one-arm-dumbbell-row
- **Movement pattern:** single-arm row, one knee and hand braced on a bench.
- **Equipment:** flat bench, dumbbell.
- **Camera angle:** side-front view, braced position and pulling arm visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** pull dumbbell to the hip over 1.5 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps per side (film one side).
- **Key form cues:** flat back parallel to the floor, pull toward the hip (not the chest), no torso rotation.
- **Must be visible:** the stable braced spine and the elbow driving past the torso.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** twisting the torso, shrugging the shoulder, dropping the head.

### 10. seated-cable-row
- **Movement pattern:** seated horizontal row on a cable machine.
- **Equipment:** cable row station, V-handle or straight bar.
- **Camera angle:** side view, full torso and cable travel visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** pull handle to the navel over 1.5 s, extend arms over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** tall chest, slight knee bend, torso nearly still (no rocking), squeeze shoulder blades together.
- **Must be visible:** the scapular squeeze at full contraction.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** leaning far back and forth, rounding the shoulders forward at the stretch.

### 11. face-pull
- **Movement pattern:** cable face pull to forehead height (rear delts / upper back).
- **Equipment:** cable station with rope attachment, pulley at upper-chest height.
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, rope path and elbows visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** pull rope toward the forehead over 1.5 s, externally rotating so knuckles face up; control back over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** elbows high and wide, rope ends split around the ears, pause with knuckles up.
- **Must be visible:** the external rotation at the end range.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** pulling to the chest, elbows dropping, leaning back.

## Shoulders

### 12. dumbbell-overhead-press
- **Movement pattern:** bilateral overhead press, standing.
- **Equipment:** pair of dumbbells.
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, full press path visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** press from shoulder height to overhead over 1.5 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** ribs down (no excessive arch), dumbbells finish over the shoulders with biceps by the ears, full lockout without shrugging.
- **Must be visible:** the stacked wrist-elbow-shoulder line at lockout.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** arching the lower back, pressing in front of the face, half reps.

### 13. barbell-overhead-press
- **Movement pattern:** standing barbell overhead press.
- **Equipment:** barbell (from a rack).
- **Camera angle:** side view showing head clearance and bar path.
- **Motion path / tempo:** press from upper chest to overhead over 1.5 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** squeeze glutes, move the head slightly back then through ("head through the window"), bar finishes over mid-foot.
- **Must be visible:** the vertical bar path and the head-through position at lockout.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** leaning back excessively, pressing around the chin, soft lockout.

### 14. lateral-raise
- **Movement pattern:** dumbbell lateral raise (side delts).
- **Equipment:** pair of light dumbbells.
- **Camera angle:** front view, arm abduction path visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** raise arms to shoulder height over 1.5 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** slight elbow bend, lead with the elbows, stop at shoulder height, no swinging.
- **Must be visible:** the controlled arc and the pause at the top.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** shrugging, using momentum, raising above shoulder height.

### 15. front-raise
- **Movement pattern:** dumbbell front raise (front delts).
- **Equipment:** pair of light dumbbells.
- **Camera angle:** side view, arm raise path visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** raise one or both arms to shoulder height over 1.5 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** torso still, arms straight with a soft elbow, stop at shoulder height.
- **Must be visible:** the still torso (no lean-back).
- **Faults to avoid showing:** swinging the weights up, arching the back, raising overhead.

### 16. dumbbell-shrug
- **Movement pattern:** dumbbell shrug (upper traps).
- **Equipment:** pair of dumbbells (moderate-heavy).
- **Camera angle:** front view, shoulder elevation visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** shrug shoulders straight up toward the ears over 1 s, hold briefly, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** arms straight at the sides, pure vertical shrug, brief squeeze at the top.
- **Must be visible:** the straight up-down path (no rolling).
- **Faults to avoid showing:** rolling the shoulders, bending the elbows, neck craning forward.

## Arms

### 17. barbell-curl
- **Movement pattern:** standing barbell biceps curl.
- **Equipment:** barbell (straight or EZ).
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, elbow position visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** curl to shoulder height over 1.5 s, lower over 2–3 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** elbows pinned at the sides, torso still, full stretch at the bottom.
- **Must be visible:** the motionless elbows and the slow eccentric.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** swinging the torso, elbows drifting forward, partial reps.

### 18. dumbbell-bicep-curl
- **Movement pattern:** alternating or simultaneous dumbbell curl, standing.
- **Equipment:** pair of dumbbells.
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view.
- **Motion path / tempo:** curl over 1.5 s with a slight supination at the top, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** elbows pinned, wrists neutral, control both dumbbells independently.
- **Must be visible:** the supination (palms turning up) and even left/right tempo.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** swinging, one arm rushing ahead, wrists bending back.

### 19. hammer-curl
- **Movement pattern:** neutral-grip dumbbell curl (brachialis/brachioradialis).
- **Equipment:** pair of dumbbells.
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, grip orientation visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** curl with palms facing each other over 1.5 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** neutral grip maintained throughout, elbows pinned, no swinging.
- **Must be visible:** the palms-in grip from start to finish.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** rotating into a supinated curl, swinging the weights.

### 20. overhead-tricep-extension
- **Movement pattern:** overhead dumbbell triceps extension, standing or seated.
- **Equipment:** single dumbbell (both hands) or cable.
- **Camera angle:** side view, elbow and arm path visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** lower the weight behind the head over 2 s, extend overhead over 1.5 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** elbows point up and stay close to the head, only the forearms move.
- **Must be visible:** the stationary upper arms and the deep stretch.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** elbows flaring wide, arching the lower back, partial range.

### 21. tricep-pushdown
- **Movement pattern:** cable triceps pushdown.
- **Equipment:** cable station, straight bar or rope, pulley at top.
- **Camera angle:** side 45° view, elbow position visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** press down to full extension over 1.5 s, control up over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** elbows pinned at the ribs, only forearms move, squeeze at full extension.
- **Must be visible:** the pinned elbows and the lockout squeeze.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** elbows drifting forward, leaning the whole body into it, half reps.

### 22. bench-dip
- **Movement pattern:** triceps dip with hands on a bench behind, feet on the floor (or elevated).
- **Equipment:** sturdy bench.
- **Camera angle:** side view, shoulder and elbow angles visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** lower until upper arms are parallel to the floor over 2 s, press up over 1.5 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** shoulders down away from ears, elbows track back (not flaring), chest up.
- **Must be visible:** the controlled depth and the elbow path.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** shrugging, dropping too deep with shoulders rolling forward, bouncing.

## Legs

### 23. bodyweight-squat
- **Movement pattern:** bilateral squat, bodyweight.
- **Equipment:** none.
- **Camera angle:** side view, full body in frame.
- **Motion path / tempo:** descend over 2 s to thighs parallel (or below), drive up over 1.5 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** chest up, knees track over toes, weight through mid-foot/heels, neutral spine.
- **Must be visible:** the knee tracking and the depth.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** knees caving inward, heels lifting, rounding the lower back.

### 24. barbell-back-squat
- **Movement pattern:** barbell back squat (high or low bar).
- **Equipment:** barbell, squat rack, plates.
- **Camera angle:** side 45° view, bar path and depth visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** unrack, brace, descend over 2–3 s, drive up over 1.5 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** bar over mid-foot, big breath and brace, knees track over toes, hips and chest rise together.
- **Must be visible:** the vertical bar path and the braced torso.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** knees caving, "good-morning" squat (hips shooting up), shallow depth.

### 25. goblet-squat
- **Movement pattern:** squat holding a dumbbell/kettlebell at the chest.
- **Equipment:** dumbbell or kettlebell.
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, weight position and torso visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** descend over 2 s, drive up over 1.5 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** weight tucked at the chest, elbows between the knees at the bottom, upright torso.
- **Must be visible:** the counterbalance effect — upright torso with the weight at the chest.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** weight drifting forward, knees caving, heels lifting.

### 26. bulgarian-split-squat
- **Movement pattern:** rear-foot-elevated split squat.
- **Equipment:** bench or box for the rear foot, dumbbells optional.
- **Camera angle:** side view, both legs and torso visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** lower over 2 s until the front thigh is parallel, drive up over 1.5 s; 3 reps per leg (film one).
- **Key form cues:** torso slightly forward or upright, front knee tracks over the toes, weight through the front heel.
- **Must be visible:** the long stance and the vertical front-shin position.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** front knee shooting past the toes excessively, pushing off the rear foot, losing balance.

### 27. walking-lunge
- **Movement pattern:** alternating forward walking lunge.
- **Equipment:** none (dumbbells optional — film bodyweight).
- **Camera angle:** side view tracking the walk, full stride visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** step forward, lower over 1.5 s, drive through the front heel to the next step; 3 steps per leg continuous.
- **Key form cues:** long stride, torso tall, front knee over the ankle, back knee hovers above the floor.
- **Must be visible:** the stride length and the tall torso.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** short choppy steps, front knee collapsing inward, torso leaning forward.

### 28. romanian-deadlift
- **Movement pattern:** hip hinge with barbell/dumbbells (hamstrings/glutes).
- **Equipment:** barbell or pair of dumbbells.
- **Camera angle:** side view — the hinge is everything.
- **Motion path / tempo:** hinge back over 2–3 s until a hamstring stretch (bar just below knees), drive hips forward over 1.5 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** soft knees, flat back, weight close to the legs, feel the hamstring stretch — this is NOT a squat.
- **Must be visible:** the hip hinge with minimal knee bend and the close bar path.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** rounding the lower back, squatting the movement, letting the weight drift forward.

### 29. leg-press
- **Movement pattern:** seated leg press (machine).
- **Equipment:** leg-press machine, seat/backrest adjusted so knees start near 90°.
- **Camera angle:** side 45° view, knee angle and foot placement visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** lower the sled over 2 s, press over 1.5 s without locking hard; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** feet flat and shoulder-width on the platform, lower back pressed into the pad, never fully lock the knees.
- **Must be visible:** the foot position and the controlled (non-locking) extension.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** knees locking out, lower back peeling off the pad, hands pushing on the knees.

### 30. standing-calf-raise
- **Movement pattern:** standing calf raise (gastrocnemius).
- **Equipment:** step or machine; bodyweight or dumbbell.
- **Camera angle:** side view, ankle range visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** rise onto the toes over 1.5 s, pause, lower below step level over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** full stretch at the bottom, tall pause at the top, no bouncing.
- **Must be visible:** the full ankle range — stretch below, peak above.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** bouncing, bending the knees, partial range.

## Core

### 31. plank
- **Movement pattern:** prone isometric hold on forearms and toes.
- **Equipment:** mat.
- **Camera angle:** side view, full body line visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** hold a rigid position — film a continuous 20–30 s hold (clip may be trimmed).
- **Key form cues:** straight line head→heels, glutes and abs braced, forearms under shoulders, steady breathing.
- **Must be visible:** the unbroken body line and the braced midsection.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** sagging hips, piked hips, holding the breath.

### 32. bicycle-crunch
- **Movement pattern:** supine alternating elbow-to-knee crunch.
- **Equipment:** mat.
- **Camera angle:** side 45° view, torso rotation visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** slow alternating reps — 2 s per side; 3 per side continuous.
- **Key form cues:** lower back pressed into the mat, rotate the ribcage (not just the elbows), extend the opposite leg fully.
- **Must be visible:** the torso rotation and the extended leg.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** yanking the neck, rushing, lower back arching off the mat.

### 33. russian-twist
- **Movement pattern:** seated torso rotation, feet grounded or lifted.
- **Equipment:** mat; medicine ball or dumbbell optional (film with light weight).
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, rotation range visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** rotate side to side over ~1.5 s per side; 3 per side continuous.
- **Key form cues:** tall chest, slight lean back, rotate the whole torso (shoulders, not just arms), controlled — no bouncing.
- **Must be visible:** the shoulder-driven rotation and the stable hips.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** rounding the back, swinging the arms without rotating the torso, holding the breath.

### 34. dead-bug
- **Movement pattern:** supine contralateral limb extension (deep core).
- **Equipment:** mat.
- **Camera angle:** side view, lower-back contact visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** extend opposite arm/leg over 2 s, return over 2 s; 3 per side continuous.
- **Key form cues:** lower back FLAT on the mat the entire time, ribs down, move slowly.
- **Must be visible:** the unmoving lower back while limbs extend.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** lower back arching as the leg extends, rushing, holding the breath.

### 35. hanging-knee-raise
- **Movement pattern:** hanging knee raise (lower abs/hip flexors).
- **Equipment:** pull-up bar.
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, hang and knee lift visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** raise knees to hip height over 1.5 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** dead hang start, posterior pelvic tilt to lift (not just hip flexors), no swinging.
- **Must be visible:** the still torso and the controlled lift.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** swinging/kipping, partial lifts, dropping the legs.

### 36. ab-wheel-rollout
- **Movement pattern:** kneeling ab-wheel rollout (advanced anti-extension).
- **Equipment:** ab wheel, mat for knees.
- **Camera angle:** side view, full extension range visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** roll out over 2–3 s to near-full extension, pull back over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** braced abs, no lower-back sag at full extension, only roll as far as the back stays flat.
- **Must be visible:** the flat back at maximum reach.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** sagging the lower back, collapsing at the bottom, rolling too far.

### 37. superman
- **Movement pattern:** prone back extension (lower back/glutes).
- **Equipment:** mat.
- **Camera angle:** side view, lift height visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** lift arms, chest, and legs over 2 s, hold 1 s, lower over 2 s; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** squeeze the glutes, lift the chest (not just the head), keep the neck neutral.
- **Must be visible:** the simultaneous arm/leg lift with a neutral neck.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** craning the neck up, jerking, holding the breath.

## Conditioning

### 38. burpee
- **Movement pattern:** squat → plank → push-up → jump (full-body conditioning).
- **Equipment:** none.
- **Camera angle:** side view, full sequence visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** continuous flowing reps, ~3 s per rep; 3 reps.
- **Key form cues:** chest to floor on the push-up, flat back in the plank jump-back, soft landing on the jump with arms overhead.
- **Must be visible:** the chest touching the floor and the full jump at the top.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** skipping the chest-to-floor, sagging in the plank, hard stompy landings.

### 39. mountain-climber
- **Movement pattern:** plank-position alternating knee drives.
- **Equipment:** mat.
- **Camera angle:** side view, plank line and knee drive visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** steady controlled pace — not a sprint; 3 drives per leg continuous.
- **Key form cues:** rigid plank line, drive knees toward the chest, hips stay level (no bouncing).
- **Must be visible:** the stable hips and the full knee drive.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** hips piking up and down, sagging lower back, partial knee drives.

### 40. jumping-jacks
- **Movement pattern:** bilateral jump with arms overhead.
- **Equipment:** none.
- **Camera angle:** front view, full body visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** steady rhythmic pace; 5 reps continuous.
- **Key form cues:** soft landings on the balls of the feet, full arm extension overhead, slight knee bend on landing.
- **Must be visible:** the full arm range and the soft landings.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** stiff-legged landings, half arm raises, landing flat-footed hard.

### 41. high-knees
- **Movement pattern:** running in place with high knee drive.
- **Equipment:** none.
- **Camera angle:** side 45° view, knee height visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** quick but controlled cadence; 5 drives per leg continuous.
- **Key form cues:** knees to hip height, tall posture, pump the arms, land softly on the forefoot.
- **Must be visible:** the hip-height knee drive and the upright torso.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** leaning back, low lazy knee lifts, heel-stomping.

### 42. jump-rope
- **Movement pattern:** basic bounce step with a jump rope.
- **Equipment:** jump rope (sized so handles reach the armpits standing on the rope).
- **Camera angle:** front 45° view, rope turn and footwork visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** steady rhythm, small 1–2 inch hops; 8–10 jumps continuous.
- **Key form cues:** elbows close to the body, wrists turn the rope, soft small bounces.
- **Must be visible:** the wrist-driven rope turn and the minimal jump height.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** big jumps, arms windmilling wide, landing flat-footed.

### 43. treadmill-run
- **Movement pattern:** steady running on a treadmill.
- **Equipment:** treadmill.
- **Camera angle:** side view showing the belt, stride, and posture.
- **Motion path / tempo:** easy conversational pace; film a continuous 15–20 s segment.
- **Key form cues:** tall posture, slight forward lean from the ankles, mid-foot strike under the body, relaxed shoulders.
- **Must be visible:** the foot landing under the hips (not overstriding).
- **Faults to avoid showing:** overstriding with heel strike far ahead, hunching, holding the handrails.

### 44. stationary-bike
- **Movement pattern:** seated cycling at moderate resistance.
- **Equipment:** stationary/exercise bike, seat at hip height when standing beside it.
- **Camera angle:** side view, full pedal stroke and posture visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** steady 70–90 rpm cadence; film a continuous 15–20 s segment.
- **Key form cues:** slight knee bend at the bottom of the stroke, tall torso, light grip on the handlebars.
- **Must be visible:** the smooth circular pedal stroke and the knee angle.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** seat too low (knees over-bending), bouncing in the saddle, death-gripping the bars.

## Mobility / Recovery

### 45. cat-cow
- **Movement pattern:** quadruped spinal flexion/extension flow.
- **Equipment:** mat.
- **Camera angle:** side view, full spinal wave visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** slow breath-linked flow — 4 s per phase; 3 full cycles continuous.
- **Key form cues:** hands under shoulders, knees under hips; cow = tailbone up, chest forward; cat = tuck tailbone, round the back, let the head hang.
- **Must be visible:** the sequential wave through the whole spine (not just the neck).
- **Faults to avoid showing:** rushing, moving only the neck, collapsing the shoulders.

### 46. childs-pose
- **Movement pattern:** static kneeling rest/stretch (lats, lower back).
- **Equipment:** mat.
- **Camera angle:** side 45° view, arm reach and hip position visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** settle into the pose and hold — film a continuous 15–20 s hold.
- **Key form cues:** hips sink toward the heels, arms reach long forward, forehead rests down, breathe into the back.
- **Must be visible:** the long spine and the relaxed shoulders.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** perched hips high in the air, shrugged tense shoulders, holding the breath.

### 47. hamstring-stretch
- **Movement pattern:** standing or supine hamstring stretch (film the supine strap version for clarity).
- **Equipment:** mat, strap or towel.
- **Camera angle:** side view, leg angle and straight knee visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** ease into the stretch and hold — film a continuous 15–20 s hold per leg (one leg shown).
- **Key form cues:** straight (not locked) lifted knee, opposite leg flat, gentle pull — mild tension, never pain.
- **Must be visible:** the straight lifted leg and the relaxed shoulders.
- **Faults to avoid showing:** bouncing into the stretch, bending the lifted knee, arching the lower back off the mat.

### 48. hip-flexor-stretch
- **Movement pattern:** half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch.
- **Equipment:** mat or pad for the knee.
- **Camera angle:** side view, hip position visible.
- **Motion path / tempo:** ease into the lunge and hold — film a continuous 15–20 s hold per side (one side shown).
- **Key form cues:** tall torso, slight posterior pelvic tilt (tuck the tailbone), gentle shift forward until a stretch in the front of the back-leg hip.
- **Must be visible:** the tucked pelvis and the upright torso.
